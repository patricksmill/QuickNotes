QuickNotes helps you capture, organize, and review ideas quickly on your Android device.

Key features:
- Create, edit, pin, and delete notes with instant search across titles, content, and tags.
- Filter your list with tag chips to focus on what matters right now.
- Auto-tag notes from keywords on-device, or enable optional AI suggestions with your own API key.
- Choose OpenAI, Claude, Google Gemini, or any OpenAI-compatible endpoint in Settings.
- Schedule reminder notifications so you never miss an important thought.
- Swipe to delete, pull to refresh, and sort notes the way you work.

QuickNotes is free and open source (Apache 2.0). Your notes stay on-device unless you choose to call an external AI API with credentials you control. Internet access is only needed for optional AI-assisted tagging.
